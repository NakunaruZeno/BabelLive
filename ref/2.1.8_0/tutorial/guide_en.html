ViiTor Translate
Provides bilingual subtitle function in many scenes.
  • Step 1: Launch the
    plugin.
    1. Open the video website.
    2. Click the Chrome plugin.
    3. In the drop-down menu, find "ViiTor Translate”, click and enable the plugin.
  • Step 2: Select subtitle
    language.
    1. Click the language selector at the top.
    2. Select your desired subtitle language.
  • Step 3: Start using the
    subtitles!
    1.Play videos in the browser.
    2.Keep the ViiTor plugin running.
    3.Provide bilingual subtitles in real time.
